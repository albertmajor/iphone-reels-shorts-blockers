# Adding or correcting an app

Anyone can suggest an app or a correction, including the developers of the apps listed. That includes developers of apps that compete with StillTabs.

## What gets listed

An app is listed if it:

- is on the App Store for iPhone, and
- removes or limits Reels, Shorts or other short-video feeds (whole-app blockers go in the whole-app section).

Every app gets the same columns. We don't rank by who made it, and we don't accept payment for placement.

## How to suggest one

Open an issue or a pull request with:

- the App Store link,
- which type it is (own app, Safari extension, screen broadcast, whole-app blocker),
- price and in-app purchases as shown on the US App Store,
- the App Privacy label,
- whether DMs keep working.

If you edit the table in `README.md`, please update the matching row in `data/apps.csv` too. We check every fact against the App Store page before merging, usually within a week.

## Corrections

If a price, label or description is wrong, open an issue with a link to where the right information is. Corrections come first.
