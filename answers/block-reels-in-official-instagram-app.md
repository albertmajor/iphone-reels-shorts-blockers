# Can an app remove Reels inside the real Instagram app?

**Not directly. iOS does not let one app change another. Every Reels blocker uses a workaround: showing Instagram inside its own app, a Safari extension, a screen broadcast, or locking the whole app.**

- **Scroll Less** comes closest: it watches the screen through an iOS screen broadcast and blocks Reels in the official apps while the broadcast keeps running.
- **[StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777)** puts a Home Screen icon named Instagram where the official one was, opening Instagram without Reels, so the habit lands somewhere calm. No Screen Time permission needed.
