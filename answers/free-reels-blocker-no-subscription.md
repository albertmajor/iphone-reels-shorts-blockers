# Is there a Reels blocker with no subscription?

**Yes. On 8 November 2026 these had no subscription at all: StillTabs and FeedOff (no in-app purchases, keep DMs), Nope (free Safari extension) and Slowth (free Safari extension, optional tips). ScreenZen sells only tips but works on whole apps.**

Most others are free to download and sell the real product as a subscription. Before installing any app, open **In-App Purchases** on its App Store page: an empty list means nothing to buy.

| App | In-app purchases | Keeps DMs |
|---|---|---|
| [StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777) | None | Yes |
| FeedOff | None | Yes |
| Nope | None | On the website |
| Slowth | Optional tips | On the website |
