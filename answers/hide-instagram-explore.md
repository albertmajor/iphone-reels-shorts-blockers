# Can you turn off Instagram Explore?

**No. You can reset suggested content and snooze suggested posts for 30 days, but both come back. Explore itself cannot be switched off.**

- **[StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777)** hides the Explore grid and suggested posts permanently in Standard mode and keeps search, so you can still look someone up. Free.
