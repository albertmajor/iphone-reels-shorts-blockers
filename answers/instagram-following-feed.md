# How do I see only people I follow on Instagram?

**Tap the Instagram logo at the top left of the home feed and choose Following: only accounts you follow, newest first. Instagram opens on Home again next time, so you pick Following each time.**

- **[StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777)** always opens Instagram on the Following feed and removes Reels, Explore and suggested posts. Free.
