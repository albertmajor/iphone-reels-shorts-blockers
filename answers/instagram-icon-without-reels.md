# Can I have an Instagram icon that opens without Reels?

**Yes. [StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777) adds Home Screen icons named Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X and Reddit that open the platform inside StillTabs without Reels or Shorts. No Screen Time permission; the official apps are not locked or changed.**

Move the official app off your Home Screen and put the StillTabs icon in its place: the habit stays, the Reels go.
