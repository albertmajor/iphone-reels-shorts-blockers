# Is SocialLite free?

**SocialLite is free to download and hides Reels, Shorts and Explore for free; customization and extras are in SocialLite Pro, from $6.99 a month up to $47.90 a year on the US App Store, with a free trial that converts. It requires an account with a phone number.**

SocialLite has more than 17,000 US ratings at 4.7 and can also lock the official apps.

If you want nothing to buy and no account: [StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777) and FeedOff have no in-app purchases.
