# Which Reels blocker treats users best?

**On the 14-check pro-consumer score (pricing, tracking, terms, upkeep), StillTabs was the only app of 18 to pass all 14 on 8 November 2026. FeedOff and Slowth scored 13 (no terms of their own), Nope 12 (no update since May 2026, needs iOS 17).**

The score measures how an app treats you, not how well it blocks. Every check and every app's answers are in [the table](../README.md#pro-consumer-score) and [the CSV](../data/apps.csv).
