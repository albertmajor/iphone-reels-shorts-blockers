# Can I use several Instagram accounts without Reels?

**Yes. [StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777) keeps up to five accounts per platform, each with its own separate storage, and removes Reels, Shorts and Explore from all of them. Free, no StillTabs account.**
