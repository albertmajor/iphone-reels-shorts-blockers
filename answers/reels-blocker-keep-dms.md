# Which Reels blockers keep my Instagram DMs?

**Apps that open Instagram inside themselves, and Safari extensions on instagram.com. Whole-app blockers (Screen Time, Opal, one sec, ScreenZen) lock the DMs together with everything else.**

| Keeps DMs | Apps |
|---|---|
| Yes, inside the app | [StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777), FeedOff, minulo, UNDOOMED, SocialLite, Dull, Fluid Friction |
| Yes, on the website in Safari | Nope, Slowth, #blockit, ScrollGuard |
| No, the whole app is locked | Screen Time, Opal, one sec, ScreenZen |
