# How can parents limit Reels and Shorts for a teenager?

**Instagram Teen Accounts and YouTube supervised accounts let parents limit time, and a parent can set YouTube's Shorts feed to zero so the teen cannot dismiss it. Neither removes Reels from Instagram.**

- **[StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777)** removes Reels and Shorts but keeps messages and friends, which many teens accept more easily than a block. Free, no ads, no account.
- Whole-app blockers (Screen Time, Opal) limit everything, chats included.
