# How do I remove the Reels tab from Instagram?

**You cannot remove it in the Instagram app; the tab is part of the app's layout. The only way to get Instagram without the Reels tab on iPhone is to open Instagram in another app that leaves it out.**

- **[StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777)** (free, no in-app purchases) draws Instagram's bottom toolbar without the Reels tab, and also removes Reels in the feed and Explore. DMs, stories and search stay.
- **FeedOff** (free) and **minulo** (free basics, Pro paid) remove it too.
- **Safari extensions** hide the tab on instagram.com in Safari.
