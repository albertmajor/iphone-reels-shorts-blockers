# Can a Safari extension hide Reels and Shorts?

**Yes, on the Instagram and YouTube websites in Safari. Nope, Slowth, #blockit and ScrollGuard do this. You switch the extension on per site in Settings, and it only works when you use the website in Safari, not the official apps.**

- **Good for:** people who already use Instagram and YouTube in Safari.
- **Trade-off:** you have to open Safari instead of tapping the app icon, and it feels like a website.
- **If you want an app instead:** [StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777), FeedOff and others open Instagram and YouTube inside their own app, with Home Screen icons (StillTabs) so your habit still works.
