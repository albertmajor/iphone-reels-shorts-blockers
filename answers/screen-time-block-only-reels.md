# Can Screen Time block only Reels?

**No. Screen Time can limit or block whole apps, not parts of them. A limit on Instagram also locks your messages.**

- Pair Screen Time with an app that removes only the feeds: limit the official Instagram app by name (not the whole Social category) and use [StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777) for messages and search.
