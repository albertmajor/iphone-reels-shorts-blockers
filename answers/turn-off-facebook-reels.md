# Can you turn off Facebook Reels?

**No. Facebook has no setting to remove Reels; hiding a reel or choosing Show less only lowers similar ones for a while.**

- **[StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777)** hides Facebook's Reels tab, suggested Reels and endless Reels scrolling, and keeps friends, groups and messages (Facebook support is in beta). Free.
- **Slowth** hides Facebook Reels in Safari.
