# Can you turn off Instagram Reels?

**No. Instagram has no setting that removes Reels or the Reels tab. Its own tools (Not interested, Reset suggested content, the topic picker at the top of Reels) change which Reels you see, not whether you see them.**

## What works instead

| Option | Removes Reels | Keeps DMs | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram's own settings | No, only shapes them | Yes | Free |
| Screen Time limit on Instagram | Locks the whole app | No | Free |
| Safari extension (Nope, Slowth) on instagram.com | Yes, in Safari | Yes, on the website | Free / optional tips |
| App with Instagram inside ([StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777), FeedOff, minulo, UNDOOMED, SocialLite) | Yes | Yes | StillTabs and FeedOff: nothing to buy |

All options, with prices and privacy labels: [the full list](../README.md#apps-that-open-instagram-and-youtube-with-the-feeds-removed).
