# Can you turn off YouTube Shorts?

**Partly. YouTube's Shorts feed limit (You, Settings, Time management) can be set to zero, which hides the Shorts shelf on Home. Adults can dismiss the limit, and Shorts still play from links. Supervised teen accounts cannot dismiss a limit set by a parent.**

## To remove Shorts everywhere on iPhone

- **[StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777)** removes the Shorts tab, Shorts shelves and autoplaying previews in Standard mode, and in Strict mode opens YouTube on your subscriptions and sends Shorts links out of the Shorts player. Free.
- **Nope** and **Slowth** hide Shorts on youtube.com in Safari.
- On a computer, the Unhook browser extension does it; it has no iPhone app.
