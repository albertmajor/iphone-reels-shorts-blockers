# Can YouTube open on my subscriptions instead of Home?

**Not with YouTube's own settings. The closest is turning off watch history, which empties Home recommendations once there is no recent history, but it also switches off history you may want.**

- **[StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761367777)**'s Strict mode opens YouTube on your subscriptions, removes Shorts from search and channels, and keeps your history. Free.
- **Nope** can open youtube.com on Subscriptions in Safari.
